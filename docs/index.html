Behind the Books

Two names. One shared appetite for trouble.

Lissette Irons and Theron Vale write grown-up fantasy where romance matters, danger has consequences, and ordinary people discover that impossible worlds have been waiting for them. Their stories move from haunted Ohio homes to hidden dragon sanctuaries, distant planets, moonlit gates, and trains that refuse to obey time. At the center, there is always someone fighting for love, freedom, family, or a place to call home.

“Give us a locked door, a stubborn woman, and one ancient secret. We’ll take it from there.”
